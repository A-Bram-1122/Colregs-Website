About This Portal

The COLREGS Learning Portal is an educational resource designed to help maritime professionals and students master the International Regulations for Preventing Collisions at Sea.

Feedback & Suggestions

We welcome your feedback to improve this learning platform. If you have suggestions for new features, find errors in the content, or have ideas for improvement, please let us know.

Technical Support

If you experience technical issues with the website:

  • Ensure you are using a modern, updated web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
  • Check that JavaScript is enabled in your browser
  • Try clearing your browser cache and localStorage
  • Ensure your browser allows localStorage for this website

Content Questions

This website presents the COLREGS rules as adopted by the International Maritime Organization (IMO). For official interpretations or legal guidance, please consult:

  • The official IMO COLREGS documentation
  • Your national maritime authority
  • Qualified maritime legal professionals
  • Your maritime training institution
