Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 31, 2026

Overview

The COLREGS Learning Portal is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our website.

Data Collection

Our website operates entirely in your browser and does not collect or transmit any personal information to external servers. All data is stored locally on your device using browser localStorage.

What We Store Locally:

  • Quiz results and scores
  • Your progress through learning materials
  • Statistics about your performance

Data Storage

All data is stored in your browser's local storage. This means:

  • Data remains on your device only
  • No information is sent to our servers or third parties
  • Clearing your browser data will delete all stored information
  • Your data is not accessible across different browsers or devices

Cookies

This website does not use cookies for tracking or analytics purposes. We only use browser localStorage for saving your quiz progress and results locally.

Third-Party Services

This website does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • View your data in the Statistics section
  • Export your data as JSON from the Statistics page, or import a previously exported JSON backup (merge or replace)
  • Delete individual results or clear all data at any time
  • Clear your browser's localStorage to remove all data

Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, security depends on your device's security. We recommend:

  • Using password protection on your device
  • Keeping your browser updated
  • Not sharing your device with unauthorized users

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please visit our Contact page.
